Defrilex is expanding its global network of bilingual technical support agents for upcoming support opportunities.

This is an opportunity to join our growing international network and help users resolve technical issues with clear, professional, and culturally appropriate support in Spanish and English.

We welcome qualified candidates from around the world who are fluent in English and Spanish and comfortable working through technical problems with a customer.

01

Responsibilities

  • Provide first-line technical support in Spanish and English across phone, chat, and email.
  • Troubleshoot account access, application, and connectivity issues.
  • Guide customers through resolution steps clearly and patiently.
  • Document each interaction and ticket accurately in the client system.
  • Escalate unresolved or complex issues to the appropriate team.
  • Maintain professionalism, confidentiality, neutrality, and impartiality at all times.
  • Follow all applicable client-specific guidelines and security requirements.
  • Handle sensitive and confidential information responsibly.
  • Maintain a quiet, professional environment suitable for remote support work.
  • Complete required language proficiency and client qualification assessments.
02

Minimum requirements

  • Native or near-native fluency in English and Spanish.
  • Excellent communication, listening, and comprehension skills.
  • Ability to explain technical steps clearly to non-technical users.
  • Comfortable troubleshooting software, account, and connectivity issues.
  • Comfortable typing and navigating multiple systems while speaking with a customer.
  • Quiet and professional workspace suitable for remote support work.
  • Reliable high-speed internet connection.
  • Computer with a quality USB headset and functioning microphone.
  • Ability to pass required language proficiency and client qualification assessments.
  • Ability to maintain confidentiality and follow client-specific requirements.
03

Preferred qualifications

  • Previous technical support or help desk experience.
  • Experience with ticketing platforms such as Zendesk or similar.
  • Familiarity with common operating systems, browsers, and mobile applications.
  • Previous customer service, contact centre, or BPO experience.
  • IT or technical support certifications.

Candidates without these qualifications are still encouraged to apply.

04

Required documents

  • Updated Resume/CV.
  • Government-issued ID.
  • EF SET English Certificate or equivalent English proficiency assessment.

Optional documents

  • Technical support or IT certificates, if available.
  • Customer service or contact centre training certificates, if applicable.

Having the optional certifications may strengthen your application but is not required to apply.

05

Work type

  • 100% Remote
  • Worldwide
  • Independent Contractor
  • Flexible schedule based on client demand

Opportunities may vary based on client demand, language needs, qualifications, availability, and successful completion of required assessments.

06

Application and qualification process

  1. Application and document review.
  2. English language proficiency verification.
  3. Spanish language proficiency assessment.
  4. Technical assessment, when applicable.
  5. Client-specific qualification assessments, when applicable.
  6. Onboarding and compliance requirements.

Successful candidates will be added to the Defrilex global network and may be contacted for future support opportunities based on client demand and availability.

07

About the opportunity

Defrilex is building a reliable global network of language professionals to help bridge communication gaps between organisations and the people they serve.

If you have strong English and Spanish language skills, enjoy solving technical problems, and meet the technical and application requirements, we encourage you to apply.

Join the Defrilex global network and help make support more accessible — from anywhere in the world.
